Our story

Some things in life deserve to be made well. BOUGE began with a simple search for a beautiful bar of soap: luxurious without being extravagant, crafted with care, and a pleasure to reach for every day.

The maker

I'm Jill.

Founder & soap maker

BOUGE began with a simple search for a beautiful bar of soap, one that felt luxurious without being extravagant, crafted with care, and a pleasure to reach for every day. When I couldn't find exactly what I was looking for, I began learning how to make it myself.

What started as curiosity quickly became a quiet obsession. Every recipe refined, every ingredient carefully chosen, every detail considered, from the feel of the lather to the weight of each bar, to the way it rests in your hand.

"I believe everyday objects can elevate everyday moments."

Jill, founder of BOUGE
